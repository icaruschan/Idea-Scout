# Dan Koe AI Content Methodology

> Source: Analysis from Greg Isenberg podcast + @DreysHQ thread

## Core Philosophy

**"AI = Research Assistant, Not Writer"**

Dan Koe uses AI to deconstruct patterns and generate building blocks — but he writes the final content himself. This keeps his voice intact while leveraging AI for speed.

---

## The Content Testing System

### X as a Testing Ground

- Write **2-3 posts daily** on X
- If a tweet pops off → expand into longer content
- Same idea gets recycled across formats

**1 winning tweet = 6+ pieces of content**

### Content Recycling Flow

```
Tweet (tests the idea)
    ↓ if it performs well
Thread (adds depth)
    ↓
X Article (full breakdown)
    ↓
Newsletter / YouTube (other platforms)
```

---

## The Research Process

### Step 1: Find Top Performers

- Find 3-6 posts/videos from top performers in your niche
- Use AI to summarize hours of content into digestible notes
- Have AI compare with YOUR previous content to find connections

### Step 2: Study What Already Works

Two approaches:
1. **Your top-performing tweets** → expand into longer content
2. **Study proven winners** from others → take the TOPIC (not the content), add your unique take

> "You are iterating on proven winners"

---

## The AI Prompt System

### Three Custom Prompts (Post-Newsletter)

After writing a newsletter, run it through:

| Prompt | Output |
|--------|--------|
| **YouTube Title Prompt** | 20 titles based on best performers |
| **Deep Post Prompt** | Extracts paradoxes, quotes, psychological triggers |
| **Content Ideas Prompt** | 60 ideas based on top performers |

**Result: 1 newsletter = 80+ ideas in your Ideas Bank**

### Two-Phase Prompt Structure

**Phase 1: AI Interviews YOU**
- AI asks questions to understand your context, voice, perspective
- This captures YOUR unique angle

**Phase 2: AI Generates Content**
- Based on YOUR answers from Phase 1
- Content maintains your voice because it's grounded in your input

> "Most people skip Phase 1. That's why their content sounds generic."

## Pattern Analysis Framework

Dan doesn't just ask AI for "tweet ideas." He has AI break down **WHY** high-performing posts work:

| Analysis Dimension | What to Extract |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| **Structure** | How is the content organized? |
| **Psychology** | What emotions/triggers does it use? |
| **Hooks** | What opens grab attention? |
| **Patterns** | Common elements across winners |

Then use those patterns as **building blocks** for your own writing.

---

## The 30/70 Growth Formula

For consistent growth, follow this content mix:

| Type | Percentage | Purpose |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Proven Formats** | 30% | Your hits — formats that reliably perform |
| **Experiments** | 70% | Testing to find the NEXT winning format |

### The Rotation Cycle

1. Experiment with 70% of content
2. When a new format works → rotate it into the 30%
3. Cycle out an old proven format
4. Repeat

> "This creates a content loop system where you're always evolving"

---

## Key Mindset Shifts

### AI Outputs ≠ Final Posts

- AI gives you **deconstructed hooks, paradoxes, psychological triggers**
- You read through, pick ONE idea that sparks
- Then write the post yourself

### The Goal

Build a **content engine** that:
- Tests ideas quickly (daily posts)
- Identifies winners (tracking performance)
- Expands winners (threads, articles)
- Continuously improves (30/70 rotation)
